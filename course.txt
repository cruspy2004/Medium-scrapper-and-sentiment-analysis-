1. Markov Decision Processes (MDPs):
Concept Used: Utility and Rewards.
How It Was Used:
Articles and authors were ranked based on a computed engagement score (claps and responses). This mimics the idea of assigning rewards to states (articles) to prioritize the most impactful ones.
The system identifies the highest utility (engagement) articles/authors to target for outreach, similar to optimizing rewards in an MDP.
2. Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs):
Concept Used: Constraints and Prioritization.
How It Was Used:
A CSP involves finding solutions that satisfy constraints. In this case:
Constraints included author, claps > 0, and responses > 0 to filter valid articles/authors.
Constraints also ensured no duplicates (via drop_duplicates) and sorted by engagement_score.
The final solution identifies a list of top authors satisfying these constraints for outreach.
